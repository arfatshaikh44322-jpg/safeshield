SMART PASSWORD HABITS

Think Before You Protect

Good password security is not only about complexity. It is also about how you use, store and protect your credentials.

01 / UNIQUE

One Account, One Password

Reusing passwords increases the impact of a single compromised account. Keep important accounts protected with separate credentials.

02 / LONG

Prefer Longer Passwords

Longer passwords generally provide more combinations and can be harder to guess. Use length rather than relying only on complicated substitutions.

03 / PRIVATE

Keep Credentials Private

Never share passwords or verification codes through unexpected messages, calls or emails.
