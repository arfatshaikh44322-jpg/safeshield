SCAM ALERT • PRIZE FRAUD
🎁

You Didn't Enter.
So Why Did You Win?

Prize scams are designed to make you feel excited before you have time to think. A message promises money, a phone, a gift card or a luxury prize — then asks for your money or personal information.

🎯 Test Your Scam-Sensing Skills

🎁 How Does a Prize Scam Work?

The scam usually starts with an unexpected message claiming that you've won something. The scammer may pretend to represent a company, lottery, shopping website, celebrity or competition.


The next step is usually a request: "Pay a small fee to receive your prize."

📩 UNKNOWN MESSAGE Just now
🎉 CONGRATULATIONS! YOU'VE WON!

You have been selected as the winner of our special customer reward.

₹5,00,000

Claim your prize before the offer expires.

⚠️ Pay ₹999 processing fee to release your prize.

🎁 Claim Your Prize
🚨 STOP!

This is where the scam becomes obvious. The message is asking you to pay money before receiving an unexpected reward. A legitimate prize should not require you to hand over sensitive financial information or send money simply to "unlock" it.

🚨 Hunt For The Red Flags

Click each warning sign to see why a scammer uses it.

🎁 Unexpected Prize
If you never entered a contest, an unexpected winning message should immediately make you suspicious.
💰 Pay Before You Receive
Requests for processing fees, release fees or taxes should be independently verified.
⏰ Artificial Deadline
"Only 10 minutes left!" is designed to make you act before you think.
🔗 Suspicious Link
The link may lead to a fake website designed to steal your information.
🔑 Personal Information
Scammers may ask for your bank details, passwords, card information or identity documents.
🤫 Keep It Secret
Scammers may discourage you from checking with friends, family or the actual organization.

🔐 What Should You Do?

1. STOP
Don't click the link or contact the number provided in the message.


2. VERIFY
Find the organization's official website yourself and check whether the promotion actually exists.


3. DON'T PAY
Don't send money simply because someone claims you have won a prize.


4. DON'T SHARE
Never provide passwords, OTPs or sensitive banking information to claim an unexpected reward.

🎯 Can You Beat The Scam?

You receive this message:

🎉 "Congratulations! You have won ₹10,00,000! Pay ₹2,000 today to release your prize."

What's the safest response?

🧠 Remember This

If you didn't enter it, you probably didn't win it.

If you have to pay to receive it, stop and verify it.

Your excitement should never override your security.
