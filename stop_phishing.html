PHISHING PROTECTION

Recognize the Tricks Before They Work

Phishing relies on urgency, deception and convincing-looking messages. Knowing the common warning signs can help you stop before you respond.

🎣 Phishing Awareness Score

0%

Explore each phishing warning sign to improve your awareness score.

🎣

Suspicious Links

Phishing messages often contain links designed to send you to fake login pages or malicious websites.

Before clicking, inspect where the link actually goes. Be especially cautious with shortened URLs, unexpected attachments and links that do not match the organization they claim to represent.

⏰

Urgent Messages

Attackers may create panic by claiming your account will be closed or that you must act immediately.

Stop when a message pressures you to act immediately. Verify the situation independently using the organization's official website or a trusted contact method.

🪪

Fake Login Pages

A phishing website may copy the design of a trusted service to steal your username and password.

Check the website address carefully. Do not assume a familiar logo or professional-looking page proves that a website is legitimate.

🎁

Too-Good-To-Be-True Offers

Free prizes, unexpected refunds and unbelievable deals can be used as bait to make you click.

If an offer seems unusually generous or unexpected, pause and verify it. Avoid providing personal or payment information simply to claim a surprise reward.

🔐

Requests for Credentials

Be cautious when an unexpected message asks for passwords, verification codes or sensitive account information.

Never disclose passwords or one-time verification codes because someone unexpectedly requested them. Contact the service through a trusted channel if you need to verify a request.

🧑‍💻

Impersonation

Attackers may pretend to be banks, employers, delivery companies, friends or other trusted organizations.

Do not trust a message solely because the sender appears familiar. Verify unusual requests independently, especially requests involving money, passwords or sensitive information.

PHISHING RED FLAGS

Pause When You See These Signs

A single warning sign does not always prove a message is malicious. Multiple warning signs should make you stop and verify.

🔗

Unexpected Link

You receive a link you were not expecting or cannot explain.

🚨

Extreme Urgency

The message pressures you to act immediately or threatens consequences.

💳

Payment Request

You are unexpectedly asked for payment, banking details or financial information.

🔑

Password Request

Someone unexpectedly asks for your password, PIN or verification code.

✍️

Strange Language

Spelling, grammar, wording or formatting may feel unusual or inconsistent.

🌐

Incorrect Website Address

The address does not match the official domain you expected.

SMART PHISHING HABITS

Think Before You Click

A few simple habits can dramatically reduce the chances of falling for a phishing attempt.

01 / PAUSE

Slow Down

Urgency is one of the most common techniques used to make people act without thinking.

02 / VERIFY

Verify Independently

Use an official website, trusted phone number or another known method instead of using the contact information in a suspicious message.

03 / PROTECT

Protect Your Credentials

Never provide passwords or verification codes to unexpected requests. Use strong passwords and additional account security whenever available.

Stay One Step Ahead of Phishing.

Phishing protection works best when combined with strong passwords, account protection and safer online habits.
