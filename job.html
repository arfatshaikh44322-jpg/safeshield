Fake Job Scam

A fake recruiter can make an opportunity look too good to be true — then ask for money, personal information or sensitive documents.

🚨 STOP • VERIFY • THEN RESPOND

💼 How Does a Job Scam Work?

Scammers often pretend to represent a real company or recruitment agency. They may contact you through WhatsApp, Telegram, email, SMS, social media or fake job websites.

The offer may sound extremely attractive:

💰 High salary • 🏠 Work from home • ⚡ Immediate joining • 🎓 No experience required

The catch usually appears later — you are asked to pay money, share sensitive documents or provide financial information.

🛡️ How to Protect Yourself

1
Research the employer.
Visit the company's official website and look for genuine contact information.
2
Verify the recruiter.
Don't trust a profile simply because it uses a company logo or job title.
3
Never pay to get hired.
Be extremely cautious about registration, training or security payments.
4
Protect your documents.
Don't casually send identity or financial documents to unknown recruiters.
5
Slow down.
Scammers often create urgency so you don't have time to verify their story.
