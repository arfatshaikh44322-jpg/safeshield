Your Bank Account
Isn't Blocked.

Bank scammers create fear by pretending to be your bank and claiming that your account has suspicious activity. They want you to panic, click a link, share an OTP or reveal your banking information before you have time to think.

🎯 Test Your Scam-Sensing Skills

🏦 How Does a Bank Scam Work?

A bank scam usually begins with an unexpected SMS, email, phone call or social media message. The scammer pretends to represent your bank and claims there is a problem with your account.


They may say: "Your account has been blocked." or "Suspicious activity has been detected."


The goal is to make you panic so that you click a fake link or provide sensitive information such as your password, card details or OTP.

📩 UNKNOWN BANK MESSAGE Just now
⚠️ URGENT: ACCOUNT SECURITY ALERT

Your bank account has been temporarily restricted because suspicious activity was detected.


Your account may be permanently blocked. Verify your account immediately.

🔗 Verify Account
🚨 STOP! This message is using fear and urgency to make you act quickly. A genuine bank communication should not require you to reveal your OTP, password or banking credentials through an unexpected link. Instead, open your official banking app yourself or contact your bank through a trusted method.

🚨 Hunt For The Red Flags

Hover over each warning sign, then click it to reveal why scammers use it.

⏰ Urgent Deadline
Scammers create a short deadline to stop you from thinking carefully or checking with your bank.
🔢 OTP Request
Never share an OTP with someone who contacts you unexpectedly. An OTP can be used to authorize transactions or access accounts.
🔗 Unexpected Link
The link may lead to a fake banking website designed to steal your login details or card information.
🔑 Password Request
Your password should remain private. Never provide your banking password through a message or unexpected call.
📱 Fake Customer Support
Scammers may pretend to be bank employees and ask you to install software or share sensitive information.
😨 Fear & Panic
Threats such as "your account will be closed" are designed to make you react emotionally instead of verifying the message.

🛡️ How To Protect Yourself

Tap each safety rule after you've checked it. These green safety tips are designed to help you build safer banking habits.

⬜ Never share your OTP.
⬜ Never share your banking password.
⬜ Don't use unexpected banking links.
⬜ Open your banking app yourself.
⬜ Contact your bank through a trusted method.

🔐 What Should You Do?

1. STOP
Don't click the link or call the phone number included in the suspicious message.
2. OPEN YOUR BANK APP
Access your bank's official application yourself rather than using a link from the message.
3. VERIFY
If you're still concerned, contact your bank using the number printed on your card or another trusted source.
4. NEVER SHARE OTPs
A legitimate bank employee should not ask you to reveal a confidential OTP over an unexpected message or call.
