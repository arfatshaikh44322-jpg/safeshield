🎭 How Impersonation Scams Work

The scam usually follows a simple pattern.

1

COPY

The scammer copies someone's name, profile photo or online identity.

2

CONTACT

They send friend requests or private messages to people.

3

BUILD TRUST

They pretend to be someone you know or someone trustworthy.

4

ASK

Eventually they request money, information, codes or access.

🚩 Warning Signs

Look for several clues instead of trusting a profile based on appearance alone.

📸

Copied Profile Picture

The account uses someone else's photograph or identity.

🆕

Recently Created Account

A new account with little history deserves extra caution.

💬

Unexpected Message

Someone suddenly contacts you with an unusual request.

💰

Money Request

They suddenly need money or ask you to transfer funds.

🔢

OTP Request

They ask for a verification code or login code.

⏰

Urgency

They pressure you to act before you have time to verify.

🛡️ How To Protect Yourself

If you suspect an account is impersonating someone, follow these steps.

1

Verify Through Another Channel

Contact the real person through a phone number or account you already trust.

2

Don't Send Money

Never send money simply because a social media account asks you to.

3

Never Share OTPs

A verification code should remain private. Don't share it with anyone.

4

Check Their Profile

Look at account age, posts, followers, usernames and unusual activity.

5

Report and Block

Report suspicious impersonation accounts to the social media platform and block them.

🔐 Verify Before You Trust

A familiar name and profile picture are not proof of identity.

When someone online suddenly asks for money, passwords, OTPs or sensitive information: STOP → VERIFY → THEN ACT.
