VERIFICATION GUIDE

Know What to Look For

A trustworthy-looking design does not automatically mean a website is legitimate. Check several signals before continuing.

🔗

Inspect the URL

Look closely at the spelling and structure of the website address. Attackers may use extra words, misspellings or deceptive domain names to imitate trusted websites.

CHECK THE ADDRESS
🔒

Look for HTTPS

HTTPS encrypts the connection between your browser and a website. However, HTTPS alone does not prove that the website itself is legitimate.

CONNECTION SECURITY
🏢

Confirm the Domain

When visiting a company or service, make sure the domain matches the organization you intended to visit rather than relying only on logos or page design.

VERIFY IDENTITY
📩

Avoid Unexpected Links

Be cautious with links received through unexpected emails, messages or social media posts. When possible, navigate directly to the official website.

AVOID SHORTCUTS
⚠️

Watch for Pressure

Urgent warnings, countdowns, threats or requests for immediate payment can be signs of a scam. Slow down and verify before taking action.

STOP & THINK
🛡️

Verify Independently

If a website asks for sensitive information, verify the organization through a trusted source before signing in or submitting personal details.

DOUBLE CHECK
URL RED FLAGS

Small URL Changes Can Matter.

Scam websites may use addresses that look convincing at first glance. Always inspect the complete domain before trusting a page.

BEFORE YOU SIGN IN

Use This Quick Verification Checklist

Take a few seconds to verify a website before entering passwords, payment information or other sensitive data.

01

Check the exact domain

Make sure the website address belongs to the organization you intended to visit.

02

Look for HTTPS

Check that the connection is encrypted, while remembering that HTTPS is not proof that a site is legitimate.

03

Question unexpected links

Avoid clicking unfamiliar links from unexpected messages whenever possible.

04

Look for suspicious pressure

Be cautious when a page tries to rush you into making a decision.

05

Verify through another source

Use an independently trusted source to confirm that a website or request is genuine.

06

Stop if something feels wrong

Do not enter sensitive information when the website or request seems suspicious.

Don't Trust the Design. Verify the Website.

A professional-looking page can still be deceptive. Slow down, inspect the address and verify before sharing sensitive information.
