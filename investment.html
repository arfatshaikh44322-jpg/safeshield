INVESTMENT SCAM • HIGH RISK

Don't Let a Fake Profit Empty Your Wallet.

Investment scams promise easy money, guaranteed returns and exclusive opportunities.

The bigger the promise, the more carefully you should verify it.

📈 See How the Scam Works 🚨 View Red Flags

⚠️ STOP!

🎭 How Investment Scams Work

Hover over each stage to explore the pattern.

🎣

1. The Hook

You see an advertisement, message or social media post promising unusually high returns.

💰

2. Small Deposit

You may be encouraged to start with a small amount so the opportunity feels safe.

📊

3. Fake Profits

A fake website or app may display growing profits to convince you that your investment is working.

📈

4. Bigger Investment

Once you trust the platform, you're pushed to invest more money.

🔒

5. Withdrawal Problem

When you try to withdraw your money, unexpected fees or excuses appear.

💸

6. The Trap

The scammer may demand additional payments before disappearing with your money.

🚨 Check the Red Flags

🚨 Investment Scam Red Flags

If you notice several of these together, stop and verify.

📈 "Guaranteed" Returns

No legitimate investment can simply guarantee extraordinary profits without risk.

⏰ Limited-Time Pressure

"Invest before midnight" or "only two spots left" is designed to rush you.

💎 Unrealistic Profits

Promises of turning a small amount into a huge fortune quickly should raise suspicion.

📱 Random Social Media Contact

Be cautious when strangers unexpectedly contact you with investment opportunities.

💳 Upfront Fees

Be suspicious if you're asked to pay unexpected fees before accessing your money.

🔐 Fake Trading Platform

A professional-looking app or dashboard doesn't automatically prove that an investment is legitimate.

🛡️ Learn How to Stay Safe

🛡️ Before You Invest, Verify

🔎 Research

Research the company, platform and people behind the opportunity independently.

🧠 Understand the Investment

Don't invest in something you don't understand simply because someone promises profit.

🚫 Don't Trust Guaranteed Profits

High returns normally involve risk. Treat "guaranteed" high returns with caution.

⏸️ Take Your Time

Legitimate opportunities should not require you to make a rushed decision.

🔐 Protect Your Information

Never casually share passwords, OTPs, banking credentials or sensitive documents.

👥 Ask Someone You Trust

A second opinion can help you notice warning signs you may have missed.
