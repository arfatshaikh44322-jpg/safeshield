Stop When You See These Signs

These signals deserve your attention.

⏰ Extreme urgency or countdowns
🔐 Requests for passwords or OTPs
💰 Unexpected demands for money
🔗 Suspicious or unexpected links
🤫 Requests to keep things secret
🎁 Unrealistic rewards or profits
📞 Pressure to act immediately
🪪 Requests for sensitive identity documents
SafeShield Protocol

Give yourself 10 seconds.

Scammers rely on emotional reactions. A short pause gives you time to question the request and verify it independently.

01

STOP

Don't react immediately to pressure, threats or exciting offers.

02

THINK

Ask yourself whether the request actually makes sense.

03

VERIFY

Contact the organization or person using an independent official channel.

04

ACT

Only continue once you are confident the request is legitimate.
