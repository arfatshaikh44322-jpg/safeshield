⚠️ ROMANCE SCAM ALERT

When Trust Becomes the Trap.

Romance scammers can build emotional connections and use that trust to manipulate people into sending money or sharing sensitive information.

🔍 See How It Works

💬 How a Romance Scam Works

The relationship may feel real. The manipulation is not.

Imagine meeting someone online. They seem friendly, understanding and interested in your life.

Alex:
"I've never connected with someone like this before. I feel like I can tell you anything."
You:
"That's really sweet. I feel comfortable talking to you too."
Alex:
"I wish I could meet you. Unfortunately I'm stuck because of an emergency."
Alex:
"Could you help me with some money? I'll pay you back, I promise."
🚨 Warning: The emotional connection may be used to create pressure. Some scammers spend weeks or months building trust before asking for money.

🚩 Romance Scam Red Flags

Click a warning sign to inspect it.

💰

Money Requests

They suddenly need money for travel, medical bills or emergencies.

📹

Avoids Video Calls

They repeatedly make excuses for avoiding real-time video calls.

✈️

Never Meets

They constantly promise to meet but always have another excuse.

❤️

Very Fast Attachment

They quickly claim strong feelings or talk about a future together.

🤫

Keep It Secret

They discourage you from telling friends or family.

🪪

Personal Information

They ask for financial information, passwords or sensitive details.

🛡️ How to Protect Yourself

Five simple habits can make manipulation much harder.

1

Don't Send Money

Never send money to someone you only know through an online relationship.

2

Verify Their Identity

Use independent methods to confirm that the person is who they claim to be.

3

Talk to Someone You Trust

Friends and family may notice warning signs that emotions make difficult to see.

4

Protect Personal Information

Don't share financial details, passwords, OTP codes or identity documents.

5

Stop If Something Feels Wrong

You don't owe anyone money simply because they have built an emotional connection with you.

❤️ Real Relationships Don't Require Payments

Trust should never require you to ignore your instincts, hide the relationship or send money under pressure.
