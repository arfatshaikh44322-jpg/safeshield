Don't Wait. Report It.

If you have been targeted by a cyber scam or lost money through an online fraud, reporting quickly can help authorities and financial institutions respond sooner. Learn what to do, what information to keep, and where to report.

THE RESPONSE PLAN

What should you do after a cyber scam?

Don't panic and don't delete evidence. Follow these steps in order and report the incident as soon as possible.

01

Stop the damage

Stop communicating with the scammer. Do not send additional money, OTPs, passwords, card details or remote-access permissions.

02

Call 1930

If money has been lost through a cyber financial fraud, report it immediately through the National Cyber Crime Helpline.

03

File online

Use the official National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal to submit the relevant complaint and provide accurate incident details.

04

Save everything

Keep transaction details, screenshots, phone numbers, URLs, emails, messages and other evidence connected with the incident.

TIME MATTERS

How quickly should you report?

The safest rule is simple: report immediately. Do not wait until you have collected every piece of evidence before starting the reporting process.

⚡ Financial fraud

If money has been transferred or debited because of a cyber scam, act immediately. The official system is designed for rapid reporting of financial cyber fraud.

Immediate action is best Call 1930 as soon as possible and follow the instructions provided during the reporting process.
If you receive an acknowledgement Keep your acknowledgement or reference number safe. If the reporting process asks you to complete the online complaint after calling 1930, do so within the required timeframe shown by the official system.
⏱️

Why waiting can hurt

Digital payments can move quickly between accounts. Early reporting gives the relevant authorities, banks and financial intermediaries more opportunity to act on the transaction.

Remember You do not need to be completely certain about everything before reporting a suspected fraud.
KEEP YOUR EVIDENCE

What information should you have ready?

Accurate information makes your complaint easier to understand. Keep whatever is available — don't delay reporting simply because something is missing.

💳
Transaction details Transaction ID, date, amount and relevant payment details.
📱
Phone numbers Numbers used by the scammer, including WhatsApp or other messaging accounts.
🔗
Suspicious links Website URLs, payment links or suspicious pages involved in the scam.
💬
Messages & emails Keep screenshots, emails, chat messages and other communication.
🏦
Bank / wallet information Relevant bank, wallet, merchant or UPI information connected to the transaction.
🕒
Incident timeline Note approximately when the scam started, when you interacted and when the transaction occurred.
