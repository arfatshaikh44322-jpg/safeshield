⚠️ DIGITAL PAYMENT SCAM

The UPI & QR Code Scam

A QR code may look harmless, but scammers can use fake refunds, payment requests and social engineering to trick you into authorizing a payment.

🎭 How Does the Scam Work?

A scammer may contact you pretending to be a buyer, customer-service representative, friend, delivery agent or someone offering a refund.


They may send a QR code or a payment request and tell you that you need to scan it or enter your UPI PIN to receive money.


⚠️ Your UPI PIN is used to authorize payments. Treat any unexpected request to enter it as a major warning sign.
