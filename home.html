Threat Awareness

Don't let a scam catch you off guard.

Hover over the alerts to see how SafeShield highlights potential threats. Visit the Scams page to explore them in detail.

🎣

Phishing Attempts

Fake messages and websites may try to steal passwords, payment details or personal information.

⚠ Verify before clicking
💳

Payment Scams

Fraudsters can use fake payment requests, impersonation and urgency to manipulate victims.

⚠ Verify every payment request
🎭

Impersonation

Scammers may pretend to be banks, companies, officials, friends or family members.

⚠ Confirm their identity
💼

Fake Job Offers

Fraudulent recruiters can use attractive job offers to request money or sensitive information.

⚠ Research first
🎁

Prize Scams

Unexpected prize messages may ask for fees, account information or payment details.

⚠ Don't pay to claim a prize
📱

Social Engineering

Attackers may exploit trust, fear, curiosity or urgency to convince you to take unsafe actions.

⚠ Stop and think
