DATA BACKUP PROTECTION

Protect What You Cannot Replace

A reliable backup strategy helps reduce the impact of lost devices, damaged storage, accidental deletion and security incidents.

💾

Back Up Important Files

Identify important documents, photos, school or work files and other information that would be difficult to replace.

☁️

Use Secure Cloud Backup

Cloud storage can provide an additional copy of your files and make them available when your main device is unavailable.

🔒

Protect Backup Accounts

A backup is only useful if unauthorized people cannot easily access or delete it. Secure the accounts that hold your backups.

🔄

Back Up Regularly

A backup from months ago may not contain your newest information. Make backups a regular part of your digital routine.

🧰

Keep More Than One Copy

Relying on a single copy creates a single point of failure. Consider maintaining backups in separate locations or systems.

🧪

Test Your Backups

A backup should not simply exist. Check that files can actually be recovered when you need them.

Make Recovery Easier

SMART BACKUP HABITS

Think Before Your Data Is Lost

Good backup security is about preparing before something goes wrong rather than trying to recover everything afterward.

01 / IMPORTANT

Protect What Matters Most

Prioritize files that are difficult or impossible to replace. This gives your backup routine a clear starting point.

02 / SEPARATE

Avoid One Single Copy

Keeping important information in only one location means one failure could remove your only copy.

03 / RECOVER

Test Before You Need It

Regularly checking that your backups can be restored helps you discover problems before an emergency occurs.
