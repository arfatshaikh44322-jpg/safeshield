DIGITAL SECURITY

Build Your Personal Security Shield

A few simple habits can dramatically improve the security of your accounts, devices and personal information.

0%

Strong Passwords

Create long, unique passwords and avoid using the same password across multiple accounts.

🎣

Stop Phishing

Be cautious with unexpected emails, messages, attachments and links requesting sensitive information.

📱

Secure Your Devices

Keep your operating system, browser and applications updated with the latest security fixes.

🔑

Enable 2FA

Add another verification step to your important accounts for stronger protection.

🌐

Verify Websites

Check website addresses carefully before entering passwords, payment details or personal information.

💾

Backup Important Data

Keep copies of important files so you can recover them after loss, damage or a security incident.

SMART HABITS

Think Before You Trust

Good cybersecurity is often about slowing down and checking before acting.

01 / PAUSE

Don't Rush

Urgency is one of the most common tactics used to pressure people into making unsafe decisions.

02 / VERIFY

Check Independently

Contact organizations through official channels instead of relying on unexpected messages.

03 / PROTECT

Share Less

Only provide sensitive information when you understand who is requesting it and why it is needed.
