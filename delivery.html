DELIVERY SCAM • STAY ALERT

Your Package Is Stuck?

A message says your package cannot be delivered because your address or payment information needs updating.

It may look like a normal delivery message — but one click could lead to a fake website designed to steal your information.

📦 See How It Works

📱 It Looks Like a Normal Delivery Update

Can you spot what is wrong with this message?

📱 Messages
📦 Delivery Service

Your package could not be delivered because your address information is incomplete.

Please update your information within 2 hours to avoid returning the package.

🔗 Update Delivery Information

Sender: delivery-support@example-message.com

🚨 Danger! This is exactly how a phishing delivery scam can create urgency.

Never enter your card details, password, OTP or personal information just because a message asks you to "update" delivery information.

🔍 Can You Detect the Scam?

Test yourself before continuing.

The message says your package will be returned in 2 hours unless you click a link.

What should you do?

🚨 Delivery Scam Red Flags

Look for several warning signs at once.

⏰

Sudden Deadline

"Pay within 2 hours" or "your package will be returned" creates unnecessary pressure.

🔗

Unexpected Link

A delivery message asking you to click a random link should be treated carefully.

💳

Payment Request

Be suspicious if you're suddenly asked for card or payment information.

🔢

OTP Request

Never share an OTP with someone who contacts you unexpectedly.

🌐

Strange Website

A website that doesn't match the delivery company's official domain is suspicious.

📝

Personal Information

Requests for unnecessary personal details should make you stop and verify.

🛡️ What Should You Do?

If you receive a suspicious delivery message, follow these steps.

1

Don't Click the Link

Avoid clicking links in unexpected delivery messages.

2

Check Your Order Yourself

Open the shopping or delivery company's official app or website manually.

3

Don't Share Sensitive Information

Never provide passwords, OTPs, PINs, card details or unnecessary personal information.

4

Verify With the Official Company

Use contact information from the company's official website or application.

5

Report the Message

Report suspicious messages through your messaging service, delivery provider or appropriate authority.

📦 Your Package Can Wait.

A real delivery problem can usually be checked through the official delivery service.

Don't let a fake deadline make you give away your information.
