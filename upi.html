🚨 SCAM ALERT!

A UPI payment request is asking you to authorize a transaction.

Remember: You do NOT receive money by entering your UPI PIN. Your UPI PIN is generally used to authorize payments from your account.

🔍 How a UPI Scam Works

The scam usually follows a simple pattern.

01
📩

Contact

The scammer contacts you with a believable story.

02
😨

Create Pressure

They create fear, urgency or excitement to make you act quickly.

03
📲

Send Request

They send a payment request, QR code or suspicious link.

04
💸

Money Lost

If you authorize the transaction, money can leave your account.

🚨 Common UPI Scam Tricks

Click each card to reveal the warning sign.

📲

Fake Payment Request

Someone claims they are sending you money and asks you to approve a request.

🚨 Red Flag: Approving a UPI request can authorize money leaving your account.
🔳

QR Code Scam

A person sends a QR code and says "scan this to receive money."

🚨 Red Flag: Scanning a QR code may initiate a payment rather than receive one.
💰

Fake Refund

Someone claims that you have a refund waiting and asks you to approve a payment.

🚨 Red Flag: A refund should not require you to send money to receive money.
🏦

Fake Bank Support

Someone pretends to be bank support and asks for your UPI PIN or OTP.

🚨 Red Flag: Never disclose your UPI PIN, password or OTP to an unexpected caller.
🎁

Prize Scam

You are told that you won money but must make a UPI payment to claim it.

🚨 Red Flag: Unexpected prizes that require payment are a major warning sign.
👨‍💼

Fake Buyer Scam

A supposed buyer sends a payment request while pretending to pay for your product.

🚨 Red Flag: Never approve a payment request simply because someone claims it is a payment.

🛡️ UPI Safety Rules

Keep these rules in mind whenever you make or receive a digital payment.

🔐

Never Share Your UPI PIN

Your UPI PIN is confidential. Do not share it with callers, merchants or anyone else.

🔢

Never Share OTPs

Treat OTPs and verification codes as private security information.

🔳

Be Careful With QR Codes

Don't scan an unknown QR code just because someone says it will send money to you.

👀

Check Before Paying

Always verify the recipient, amount and transaction details before authorizing a payment.

📞

Verify Unexpected Calls

If someone claims to represent your bank, contact the bank independently.

⏰

Don't Rush

Scammers often use urgency. Take a moment to verify the request.

🚨 What If You Made a Payment to a Scammer?

Don't panic. Take action quickly.

🛡️ Act Quickly

1. Stop further communication.
Don't continue following instructions from the suspected scammer.
2. Contact your bank or payment provider.
Use an official app, website or verified contact channel.
3. Secure your accounts.
If you shared credentials or sensitive information, change them immediately.
4. Save the evidence.
Keep transaction details, messages, screenshots and other relevant information.
5. Report the incident.
Report suspicious activity through the appropriate financial institution, platform or official authority.

🔐 Remember One Thing

Receiving money and authorizing a payment are not the same thing.

Stop. Check. Verify. Then Pay.
